# Company Harness — Landscape, Direction, Gaps and Backlog Vetting

*Research date: 2026-09-10. Scope: GitHub issues #30, #40–#44 and the epics in `.manage/issues-company-harness.md`. Every claim has a source number from [Sources](#sources). If a claim rests on a secondary source only, or could not be confirmed, it is marked **(secondary)** or **(unverified)**.*

Thesis under test (owner): *"How we shape people's knowledge is how they will shape workspaces, projects, processes."* Knowledge first.

---

## Executive summary

- **"Company harness" is now a crowded category, but only on the agent-orchestration side.** Paperclip (MIT, launched March 2026, ~80k stars) models agents as a company with org charts, budgets, heartbeats and export/import of whole companies [1]. MetaGPT, ChatDev 2.0, CrewAI and the Microsoft Agent Framework cover role-based multi-agent teams [3–6]. OpenAI (Frontier, workspace agents), Microsoft (Copilot Studio + Agent 365) and Google (Gemini Enterprise, Workspace Studio) sell the enterprise version [8, 9, 22–25].
- **SMB "AI employee" products are all closed SaaS that keep your knowledge inside them.** Examples: Lindy, Relevance AI, Sintra's "Brain AI", Motion [29–32], and Anthropic's own Claude for Small Business plugin (May 2026) [17]. Autonomy-first players like Polsia raise money but draw complaints about unapproved actions [28].
- **Deploying a self-hosted business stack is solved.** Coolify v4 went stable in April 2026 with 280+ one-click services [33], and Dokploy does the same job [34]. The open-source business apps themselves are changing licenses: Cal.com went closed source in April 2026 and relaunched the open code as Cal.diy [38], and NocoDB moved from AGPL to a Sustainable Use License in January 2026 [35].
- **Direction 1: skills have replaced prompts as the unit of know-how.** Agent Skills became an open standard on 2025-12-18 [15, 19]. Anthropic (org skills), OpenAI (ChatGPT Skills), Notion (Skills, exportable as `SKILL.md`) and Vercel (skills.sh) all adopted it within about eight months [10, 16, 20, 21].
- **Direction 2: MCP is the tool layer, under neutral governance.** MCP and AGENTS.md went to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation in December 2025 [50]. The spec moves on a steady cadence (2025-11-25, 2026-07-28) [51, 52]. Most business apps now ship an MCP server: Twenty, Baserow, Coolify, Filestash, Glean, Guru [33, 36, 37, 41, 43, 44].
- **Direction 3: knowledge is moving into plain files.** Examples: Claude Cowork works inside local folders [12], Karpathy's "LLM wiki" gist [48], Obsidian's official skills [49], Basic Memory [46], Vercel eve's directory-as-agent [26], and 60k+ repos with AGENTS.md [55].
- **Direction 4: governance and supply-chain risk are now the constraint.** Snyk found security flaws in 36% of 3,984 audited skills and 76 confirmed malicious payloads [56]. OWASP started an Agentic Skills Top 10 [57]. Microsoft made Agent 365 generally available as an agent control plane in May 2026, for enterprises only [22].
- **Unclaimed opportunity 1 — knowledge-first "Space kits".** A company-type starter that seeds `docs/`, `knowledge/` and `skills/` in the team's own Drive or OneDrive, plus an AI interview skill that captures how the business works. Vendors each do a version of this, but only inside their own platform [17, 31].
- **Unclaimed opportunity 2 — trust and review for shared skills.** Tekt currently links *every* synced skill into Claude Code automatically, so anyone with write access to the shared folder can push instructions into everyone's agent. Enterprise tools solve this only inside one vendor [16, 22]. Nothing covers skills shared through ordinary cloud storage.
- **Unclaimed opportunity 3 — knowledge upkeep on plain files.** Owner and review-date checks, stale-page lint, and a plain-language "what does our AI know" report for managers. Guru sells verified knowledge as SaaS [44], and Karpathy's pattern includes a "lint" step [48], but nobody offers this for a team folder with bring-your-own-AI.
- **Unclaimed opportunity 4 (Explore) — one skill source, every AI app.** Reach Codex, opencode, Gemini CLI, Claude Desktop/Cowork and ChatGPT from one Space. The pieces exist but are developer-only or single-machine [20, 59]. Open feature requests show demand [65].
- **Verdict on the current backlog.** #41 (universal field protocol + sync engine) and #43 (app bundle deploy) should be **Skip**: others do them well, and they are the riskiest to maintain. #40 (schema) is Skip. #42 (CLI + type picker) and #30 (directory-as-agent) are **Explore**, but only after re-scoping around Spaces. #44 (open-design) is Explore as a plain catalog entry.

## 2. Direction and trends (2025–2026)

1. **Skills over prompts, and skills as the portable unit.** Anthropic published Agent Skills as an open standard, saying *"the same skill should work whether you're using Claude or other AI platforms"* [15]. OpenAI (ChatGPT Skills for workplace tiers, 2026) [10], Notion (Skills that download as `SKILL.md`) [21] and Vercel (skills.sh) [20] followed. Plugins now bundle skills with connectors for a job function: Anthropic's knowledge-work plugins [13] and Claude for Small Business [17]. **Implication:** Tekt's `skills/` folder is on the winning format. The contested ground is *who hosts and governs* the skills. Each vendor wants skills in its own admin console (Claude org skills [16], ChatGPT workspace publishing [10], Notion pages [21]).
2. **MCP is settled as the tool layer and is maturing for enterprise use.** Neutral governance (AAIF) [50], OAuth improvements and Tasks [51], a stateless core with a formal deprecation lifecycle [52], and an official registry [53]. Business apps now ship their own MCP servers [33, 36, 37, 41, 43, 44]. **Implication:** Tekt does not need its own integration layer; curating MCP servers is enough.
3. **Knowledge is moving into plain files that agents maintain.** Examples: Cowork operates on local folders [12]; Karpathy's LLM wiki (raw → wiki → schema, with ingest/query/**lint** operations) [48]; Obsidian's official agent skills [49]; Basic Memory [46]; eve's directory-as-agent [26]; AGENTS.md [55]. Research frames this as "context engineering" [71]. **Implication:** the owner's "knowledge first" thesis matches where credible builders are heading. Upkeep (lint, freshness, ownership) is the open part.
4. **"Agents as coworkers" and "company as org chart" are mainstream hype, but results lag.** Microsoft's "Frontier Firm" and "agent boss" framing (WTI 2025) [62], OpenAI Frontier [8], and Paperclip's rise [1, 2] all push this story. Counter-evidence is also strong:
   - Gartner predicts over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027 and says only about 130 of thousands of "agentic" vendors are real [61].
   - MIT NANDA reports 95% of GenAI pilots show no measurable P&L impact, while employees at 90%+ of companies use personal AI tools [60].
   - Polsia's reviews show what goes wrong when autonomy comes before governance [28].
5. **Governance and supply-chain security are now binding constraints.**
   - Snyk ToxicSkills: 13.4% of 3,984 skills have critical issues [56].
   - The ClawHavoc campaign distributed malicious skills through ClawHub [57].
   - OWASP recommends publisher-bound signing, hash-pinning, scanning at install time, and protecting `MEMORY.md` and other identity files [57].
   - Enterprise answers exist: Agent 365 [22] and Copilot Studio controls [23].
6. **Open-source business app licenses are unstable.** Cal.com closed its commercial code [38], NocoDB moved to fair-code [35], Nango is ELv2 [42], and Dokploy and Twenty carry proprietary or enterprise carve-outs [34, 37]. **Implication:** a Tekt "app bundle" would have to track upstream license changes indefinitely.
7. **Memory is becoming its own engineering discipline** (Mem0's 2026 report, Letta's tiered memory) [47]. The main split is between vendor or vector memory and human-readable file memory [46].

## 3. Gap analysis

Each gap lists: evidence of demand · who partially covers it · why it is still unclaimed at Tekt's intersection (non-developers, storage they already use, shared knowledge and skills, bring-your-own-AI, self-hosted/sovereign).

**G1 — A knowledge-first starter for a small business, in its own storage.**
- *Demand:*
  - Small-business surveys consistently name lack of expertise and "where to start" as the main barrier. Aggregators cite 50–71% of non-adopters naming expertise (aggregator figures, **not verified against the primary surveys**) [63].
  - MIT NANDA: the 5% of pilots that succeed "build systems that learn" and fit into workflows [60].
  - Anthropic built a dedicated SMB bundle [17].
- *Partial coverage:* Claude for Small Business (Claude-only; knowledge stays in SaaS connectors) [17]; Sintra Brain AI (closed SaaS) [31]; Paperclip company templates (agent org charts, developer audience) [1].
- *Why unclaimed:* every vendor wants the company's knowledge inside its own product. Nobody ships a vendor-neutral starter that lives in the customer's Drive or OneDrive and works with whichever AI they already pay for.

**G2 — Trust and review for shared skills at small-team scale.**
- *Demand:* Snyk [56], OWASP AST01 [57], ClawHavoc [57]. Claude Code users are asking for owner-approved edits to shared skills (#82720), multi-editor org skills (#60948) and local overlays on shared skills (#70660) [65].
- *Partial coverage:* Claude and ChatGPT admin consoles (Team/Enterprise tiers only, single vendor) [10, 16]; Agent 365 (enterprise) [22]; Cisco skill-scanner (a developer tool) [58].
- *Why unclaimed:* file-shared skills skip every one of those consoles. **Tekt makes this worse today:** `tekt skill link` adds every synced skill to Claude Code automatically (spaces.md "Share skills"), so one compromised or careless collaborator's Drive account becomes an injection path into every member's agent.

**G3 — Knowledge upkeep on plain files (freshness, ownership, verification).**
- *Demand:* Guru's whole business is verified, owned knowledge [44]; Glean reached $300M ARR selling enterprise context [43]; Karpathy's pattern needs a "lint" step for contradictions and stale claims [48].
- *Partial coverage:* Guru and Glean (SaaS, enterprise); Basic Memory and Obsidian (personal, single user) [46, 49].
- *Why unclaimed:* no tool treats a shared cloud-storage folder as a knowledge base with owners and review dates that any AI can read.

**G4 — One skill source reaching every AI app, without git.**
- *Demand:*
  - Claude Code #16345 (`.github/skills/`, 41 👍), #28327 (install shared skills), #56193 (`.agents/skills/` for Codex/Gemini) [65].
  - A widely shared post by a Cowork community lead calls "how do I share my Cowork skills with my team?" *"the number one question"* (post date **unverified**) [66].
  - Notion added `SKILL.md` export to local agents [21].
- *Partial coverage:* skills.sh CLI (17 agents, developer-oriented, public skills) [20]; Skills-Manager (single machine) [59].
- *Why only partly unclaimed:* the delivery mechanics are commoditizing. The team layer (Drive sync plus delivery plus review) is not.

**G5 — Human-readable shared memory.**
- *Demand:* memory is a busy category [47]; OWASP singles out `MEMORY.md` integrity [57].
- *Partial coverage:* Basic Memory (Markdown, single user) [46]; vendor memory (closed).
- *Why unclaimed:* Tekt's current shared memory is `memory.jsonl` with newest-wins conflicts (spaces.md). Non-developers cannot read or correct it.

**G6 — Plain-language oversight for small teams ("what does our AI know and do?").**
- *Demand:* only indirect. Agent 365 and Copilot Studio governance exist for enterprises [22, 23]; Paperclip dashboards exist for autonomous agents [1]. **No direct SMB demand evidence was found. Treat this as a hypothesis.**

**Not gaps (well served — do not build):**
- Self-hosted app deployment: Coolify, Dokploy [33, 34].
- Agent-company orchestration: Paperclip, CrewAI, MS Agent Framework, Symphony [1, 5, 6, 27].
- Enterprise search / company brain: Glean, ChatGPT company knowledge, Gemini Enterprise [11, 24, 43].
- Unified cross-app data APIs: Nango and commercial unified APIs [42]. n8n is already in Tekt.
- Interop protocols: MCP, A2A, AGENTS.md, Agent Skills [19, 50–55].

## 5. Recommended next 3 backlog items

### 1) Space kits with a knowledge-capture interview (N1 + N11) — *replaces #42 and #40, refines B2 and E3*

- **What:**
  - `tekt space add <name> <storage> --kit <agency|services|saas|ops|it>`, plus `tekt kit list` and `tekt kit apply <kit> [space]`.
  - A kit is a folder in the catalog. It contains:
    - `knowledge/` templates: how-we-work, glossary, customers, decisions, roles.
    - 3–5 shelf skills.
    - Recommended `tekt tool add` lines.
    - A plain-English `HARNESS.md` describing people, roles, recurring processes and which AI apps are connected.
  - Every kit includes a `capture-interview` skill. It interviews one person at a time and writes or updates `knowledge/*.md`, ending each page with an "owner / last reviewed" footer.
- **Why:** makes knowledge the first artifact a company creates, in storage it already owns, usable by any AI (G1). Reuses `space add`, `skill add` and `tool add`.
- **Deliberately does not:** deploy apps, define a YAML graph schema, or sync CRM/calendar data. `HARNESS.md` is prose with light frontmatter, not a validated manifest. Kits will be few and hand-curated, not generated.

### 2) Skill trust and review for Spaces (N2 + N5) — *new; refines D3 (security) and changes shipped `tekt skill link` behavior*

- **What:**
  - A skill that is new or changed after sync goes to a local **pending** state and is not linked into any AI app until the member runs `tekt skill review` (shows a diff and the author from storage metadata where available) and then `tekt skill trust <skill>`.
  - Trusted skills are hash-pinned in the local `.tekt-space` file. Any change drops the skill back to pending.
  - An optional scan runs if `skill-scanner` is installed.
  - Proposals live in `skills/_proposed/<name>/`. `SKILL.md` frontmatter gains an `owner:` field, and the owner promotes a proposal by moving it. `SKILL.local.md` overlays are supported for per-person additions.
  - A per-Space setting `trust: owner-only | anyone | manual` defaults to `manual`.
- **Why:** closes a real injection path that exists today. Aligns with OWASP AST01 (hash-pinning, install-time review) [57]. Makes it safe to invite non-developers to share skills (G2).
- **Deliberately does not:** run a public registry, do cryptographic publisher signing (revisit once the Agent Skills spec defines it), or replace vendor admin consoles.

### 3) Knowledge lint and Space report (N4 + N9) — *refines B3 (`harness show/validate`) and D4; supersedes #41 for now*

- **What:**
  - `tekt knowledge lint [space]`: checks `owner:` and `reviewed:` frontmatter, pages past a review window, orphan pages, broken links, near-duplicate titles, and `.conflict` files left by bisync. Offline and deterministic by default.
  - `--ai`: hands a contradiction and staleness pass to the connected AI through a shipped `knowledge-lint` skill.
  - `tekt space report [space]`: a one-page plain-language summary (what the AI can read, what is stale, who owns what, which skills and tools are active or pending, what changed this week), written to `docs/_report.md` so managers can read it in Drive.
- **Why:** upkeep is the unsolved half of file-based knowledge (G3) [44, 48]. It delivers the "Managers get a clear view" promise without a dashboard. It also detects bisync conflict leftovers — relevant because rclone warns that data loss can occur when bisync is misused [67].
- **Deliberately does not:** build a vector index or search service, sync external business apps, or rewrite knowledge without the owner's approval. The AI pass only suggests changes.

**Disposition of the rest:**
- **#43 and #41:** close as "won't build". Point to Coolify [33] and to the MCP shelf (N7) instead.
- **#30:** narrow to "agent folder convention inside a Space" (`agents/<name>/instructions.md` + `skills/`), run by the AI apps `tekt connect` already configures. Defer the runner.
- **#44:** a catalog entry is fine, outside the company-harness track.
- **N3, N6, N10:** next planning pass.
